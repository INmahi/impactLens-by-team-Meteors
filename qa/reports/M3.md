## Failures and findings

F1 (FAIL, minor) Focus ring too faint on glass. Ring is the shadcn default 50% grey 3px; glass-ui requires a focus ring visible on glass, including over bright ice. Reproduce: load, press Tab 3 times, observe South button (crop screenshot). Owner: main (shared `components/ui/button` ring token / `index.css`); the select and slider use the browser default 1px ring, also main/globe (LayerDebugControls is temporary).

F2 (FAIL, minor, temporary UI) Text contrast below 4.5:1 over a white backdrop, measured with computed colours composited over #fff:
- StatsCheck card muted text ("Stats worker check...", "Trend", "p", "Sen's slope", "n"): 2.07:1. Owner: main (`apps/web/src/App.tsx`, `text-muted-foreground` on `.glass`). Fix: use `glass-strong` and lighter text.
- Debug controls panel (`glass`, tint 0.62): title 4.30, labels "Map layer", "Frame", date 4.40. Owner: globe-engineer (`LayerDebugControls.tsx`, temporary) or switch to `glass-strong`.
Both are temporary M3 scaffolding that M4/M6 replace; the permanent overlays (legend, watermark, header chip, pole buttons) pass. Real pixels behind these panels are Canada/ocean, not ice, in the default view.

Notes (not failing the gate)
- N1 ice_mass_map is not clipped to the ice sheets: it paints near-zero (white) over all land, with blocky coarse-cell edges (~1 degree), hiding the base map over Eurasia/North America/South America (`M3-pole-north-ice_mass_map.png`, top-left of `M3-pole-south-ice_mass_map.png` shows a blocky patch over Patagonia). Greenland and Antarctica themselves are near white at frame 1, so little signal over ice. Synthetic data, so owner is pipeline-engineer (land/ice mask) and globe-engineer (decide whether to mask to the region). Not a seam or hole.
- N2 Temperature layer shows faint concentric banding around each pole (`M3-pole-south-temperature.png`), likely 8-bit colour quantisation of the smooth field or latitude-row sampling. Cosmetic. Owner: globe-engineer.
- N3 Region outlines are coarse polygons: they cut inland or over ocean at the Antarctic Peninsula, Ross/Ronne shelves and Greenland coast (`M3-select-antarctica-west.png`). Data; owner: pipeline-engineer.
- N4 Legend over true bright ice was not measured by pixel; the legend sits bottom right, normally over ocean. The computed worst-case (white behind) passes.
- N5 One 146 ms frame during drag + wheel zoom with the temperature layer (likely tile loading at zoom); everything else under 42 ms. Not reproduced elsewhere; recheck at M4 on a longer session.
- N6 Credit bar shows the "Cesium ion" logo although ion is not used (no network request is made). Cosmetic, possibly confusing for judges; owner: globe-engineer (hide the ion logo credit, keep the Cesium and GIBS attribution text).
- N7 `SYNTHETIC DATA` watermark stays visible on gap frames (correct: data are synthetic).
