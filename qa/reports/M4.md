### 2 No-data spans
Ice mass: strip hatched amber for Jul 2017 to May 2018 (wide block) and thin hatched ticks for Jun 2002, Jun 2003, Jan 2011 (single months, about 2 px wide, visible but small). 14 gap months in total. aria-valuetext reads "June 2002, no data"; the title row shows a "no data" badge; the legend says "No data for June 2002: gap in the record, map hidden." and the layer is hidden. Legend key "Data / No data" present. Temperature and ice extent: 0 gaps, no hatching. Evidence: M4qa-scroller-ice-mass-full.png, M4qa-scroller-ice-mass-nodata.png, M4qa-ice-mass-nodata-frame.png.
NOT TESTABLE: pre-2002 hatching. The strip spans only the mode's own frames (Apr 2002 onward) because no variable is selected; widening to the ice mass record (1972) depends on the variable picker (M6).

### 5 Pole toggle and sea click
Pole group has aria-pressed and descriptive labels. South view frames Antarctica well, no seam (M4qa-pole-south.png). Clicking a coastal sea outline selected it; header chip shows north_sea (M4qa-click-1010-745.png).
F1 (FAIL, minor, also item 6): the North view frames the whole Arctic with Greenland low in the frame and the scroller overlaps its southern part. At 1440x900 the tip touches the scroller edge; at 1280x720 about the lower third of Greenland is hidden behind the scroller (M4qa-pole-north-1280x720.png, M4qa-pole-north.png). Reproduce: load, press North, resize to 1280x720, press North again. Owner: globe-engineer (fit the camera with insets for the scroller and legend). Known follow-up in CLAUDE.md.
N2: the header chip shows the raw id (north_sea, greenland), not the region name from regions.json. Owner: main (App.tsx), cosmetic.

### 6 Layout
Bounding boxes of every glass panel: no pairwise overlaps, nothing off-screen, no horizontal scroll, for all three modes at 1920x1080, 1440x900, 1280x720 (M4qa-layout-1920x1080.png, M4qa-layout-1440x900.png, M4qa-layout-1280x720.png). At 1280x720 the scroller (x 260-1020, y 606-680) clears the left cards (to y 423) and the legend (to y 325). Only collision is panel over globe content (F1).

### 7 Keyboard and accessibility
Tab order (ice mass mode): Cesium credit link, North, South, Zoom in, Zoom out, map-mode radio group (one stop, arrows move inside), Run analysis, Play, Forward one month, Forward one year, slider (Back buttons are disabled at the start and skipped). All controls have accessible names; the slider has label, valuemin/max/now/text and aria-keyshortcuts. Focus ring is visible on buttons (cyan: M4qa-focus-ring-play.png, M4qa-focus-ring-south.png), on the slider and on the picker label (M4-scroller-focus-ring.png).
N4: tab order follows the DOM, so right-hand controls (poles, zoom) come before the left picker; glass-ui asks for visual order. Owner: main (App.tsx), minor.
